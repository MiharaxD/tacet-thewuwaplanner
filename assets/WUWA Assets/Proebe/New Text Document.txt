Phoebe's ascension materials are composed of the Firecracker Jewelweed, and Cleansing Conch, which you can get in Rinascita. You also need Whisperin Core materials which can be obtained from Huanglong. In addition to all of that, you will also need a total of 170,000 Shell Credits to fully upgrade her!
