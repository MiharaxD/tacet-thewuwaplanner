You only need to level up Rover once, so the materials that change for each new Attribute is for the Forte.

Rover (Havoc)'s ascension materials are composed of the Pecok Flower, Mysterious Code, and the Whisperin Core materials from echoes in Huanglong and the Black Shores. In addition, you will need a total of 170,000 Shell Credits to fully upgrade Rover (Havoc).
